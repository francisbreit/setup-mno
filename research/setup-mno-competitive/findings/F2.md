# F2: Modern PaaS platforms

## Findings

### [1] Railway charges $5/mo Hobby or $20/mo Pro for per-second billing of CPU/RAM, with Hobby including $5 of usage credit
- quote: "Hobby — $5/month with included usage. For solo developers and side projects. Pro — $20/month per seat."
- url: https://railway.app/pricing
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown
- confidence: high

### [2] Railway limits free accounts to 1 vCPU, 0.5 GB RAM, 0.5 GB volume storage per service, while Hobby allows up to 48 GB RAM / 48 vCPU per service
- quote: "Free: 1 replica, 0.5 GB RAM, 1 vCPU, 0.5 GB Volume Storage. Hobby: 6 replicas, 48 GB RAM, 48 vCPU, 5 GB Volume Storage."
- url: https://docs.railway.com/reference/pricing
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown
- confidence: high

### [3] Railway resource pricing is $10/GB/month for RAM and $20/vCPU/month for CPU, making it significantly cheaper than Render at equivalent tiers
- quote: "RAM: $10 / GB / month, CPU: $20 / vCPU / month, Network Egress: $0.05 / GB"
- url: https://docs.railway.com/reference/pricing
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown
- confidence: high

### [4] Render's Standard web service (2 GB RAM, 1 vCPU) costs $25/month as a fixed tier, versus Railway's ~$30/month for same resources billed per-second
- quote: "Standard: $25/month, 2 GB, 1 CPU. Compute charges are prorated to the second."
- url: https://render.com/pricing
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown
- confidence: high

### [5] Render Hobby plan includes only 5 GB bandwidth/month (then $0.15/GB), Pro includes 25 GB — a major cost trap for bandwidth-heavy apps
- quote: "Hobby: 5 GB bandwidth included. Pro: 25 GB bandwidth included (then $0.15 per GB)."
- url: https://render.com/pricing
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown
- confidence: high

### [6] Fly.io is pure pay-as-you-go with no base subscription fee, starting at ~$2/month for a shared-cpu-1x with 256 MB RAM
- quote: "shared-cpu-1x, 256MB: $0.0028/hour, $2.02/month. All organizations require a credit card on file."
- url: https://fly.io/docs/about/pricing/
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown
- confidence: high

### [7] Fly.io charges dedicated IPv4 addresses at $2/month and egress at $0.02/GB in North America/Europe, with a 40% discount on reserved compute blocks
- quote: "Dedicated IPv4 addresses are $2/mo. Data egress to the Internet: $0.02 per GB. Reservation: $144/year for $20/month of usage (40% discount)."
- url: https://fly.io/docs/about/pricing/
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown
- confidence: high

### [8] DigitalOcean App Platform's Free tier is limited to 3 static-site-only apps with 1 GiB data transfer each; paid tier starts at $5/month for a single container
- quote: "Free Tier: 3 apps with static sites, 1GiB data transfer allowance per app. Paid Tier starting at $5/month."
- url: https://www.digitalocean.com/pricing/app-platform
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown
- confidence: high

### [9] DigitalOcean App Platform shared 1 vCPU/1 GiB container costs $10/month, while dedicated instances cost $12/month — offering a choice between shared and dedicated CPU
- quote: "Shared (Fixed): 1 vCPU, 1 GiB, 100 GiB transfer: $10.00. Shared: 1 vCPU, 1 GiB, 150 GiB transfer: $12.00."
- url: https://www.digitalocean.com/pricing/app-platform
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown
- confidence: high

### [10] Vercel Hobby plan is free forever but limited to personal non-commercial use; Pro at $20/user/month includes $20 usage credit and is aimed at production teams
- quote: "Hobby: Free forever — personal, non-commercial use. Pro: $20/user/month with $20 of included usage credit."
- url: https://vercel.com/pricing
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown
- confidence: high

### [11] Vercel's serverless compute is priced at $0.128/hour Active CPU with 4 hours free on Hobby; the platform is frontend-focused and does not support long-running Docker containers
- quote: "Active CPU: 4 hours / month included (Hobby), $0.128 per hour (Pro). Provisioned Memory: 360 GB-hrs / month included."
- url: https://vercel.com/pricing
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown
- confidence: high

### [12] Netlify uses a credit-based pricing model with Pro at $20/month for 3,000 credits; each production deploy costs 15 credits (~$0.10/deploy)
- quote: "Pro: 3,000 credits. Production deploys: 15 credits each. Credit pack: 1,500 credits for $10."
- url: https://www.netlify.com/pricing/
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown
- confidence: high

### [13] Netlify bandwidth costs 20 credits per GB (~$0.13/GB), making it 6.5x more expensive per GB than Fly.io ($0.02/GB) and 2.6x more than Railway ($0.05/GB)
- quote: "Bandwidth: 20 credits per GB. At Pro credit rate: $0.13 per GB."
- url: https://www.netlify.com/pricing/
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown
- confidence: high

### [14] Vercel bandwidth is priced at $0.15/GB after 1 TB included on Pro, making it 7.5x more expensive per GB than Fly.io for overage traffic
- quote: "Fast Data Transfer: 1TB / month included (Pro); then starting at $0.15 per GB."
- url: https://vercel.com/pricing
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown
- confidence: high

### [15] All six PaaS platforms support Docker/container deployments, but only Railway, Render, Fly.io, and DO App Platform allow arbitrary Docker images; Vercel and Netlify are frontend/serverless-first and do not support long-running containers
- quote: "Vercel Functions running Fluid compute... Active CPU pricing" (Vercel); "Deploy from AI, Git, or API... Functions & AI models" (Netlify)
- url: https://vercel.com/pricing + https://www.netlify.com/pricing/
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown
- confidence: high

### [16] Self-hosted Docker Swarm on a single VPS ($5-20/month from Hetzner, Contabo, or OVH) can run 20-50+ apps simultaneously for the cost of a single Railway Hobby instance
- quote: N/A — synthesis from pricing data across platforms
- url: N/A
- source_type: secondary
- published: unknown
- confidence: medium

### [17] PaaS platforms impose per-service resource caps (Railway: 48 GB RAM Hobby, Render: 512 GB Custom, Fly.io: up to 256 GB RAM) that limit large monoliths but work well for microservices
- quote: "Railway Hobby max: 48 GB RAM, 48 vCPU. Render Custom: up to 512 GB RAM, 64 CPU. Fly.io: up to 128 GB RAM per machine."
- url: https://railway.app/pricing + https://render.com/pricing + https://fly.io/docs/about/pricing/
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown
- confidence: high
